# Simple Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Simple Profit Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze and compare profit performance across different product categories.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators, a category-wise profit summary, a column chart, business insights, and a conclusion to support quick and effective decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyze total profit across product categories.
- Compare average profit by category.
- Identify the highest and lowest profit-performing categories.
- Present findings through a professional Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- Charts and Data Visualization
- Data Analysis and Formatting

## Dashboard Features
- Total Profit KPI
- Top Profit Category KPI
- Highest Average Profit KPI
- Category-wise Profit Summary
- Total Profit by Product Category Chart
- Key Business Insights
- Business Conclusion

## Key Business Insights
- Technology generates the highest total profit: 663,778.73.
- Office Supplies generates the second-highest total profit: 518,473.83.
- Furniture has the lowest total profit: 286,782.25.
- Technology has the highest average profit: 65.45.
- Office Supplies has the lowest average profit: 16.58.

## Conclusion
Technology generates the highest total and average profit among the three categories. Office Supplies generates the second-highest total profit but has the lowest average profit, while Furniture has the lowest total profit.
